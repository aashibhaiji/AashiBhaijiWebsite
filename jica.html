Building a national investment portal from zero

BanglaBiz consolidates five separate One-Stop Service portals into Bangladesh’s single digital gateway for investors. I led the end-to-end product and experience design, from information architecture and decision logic through executive alignment and launch.

visit the live portal ↗
  • 5→1 OSS portals unified into one gateway
  • 4–6 weeks from design start to UAT
  • 5 IPAs integrated: BIDA, BEZA, BEPZA, BHTPA, BSCIC
  • Sep ’25 Release 1.0 launched live

01 · The challenge

Fragmentation at a national scale

This was not a website redesign, it was a national coordination platform. The goal: one digital entry point, “One ID, One Journey”, guiding every investor to the right authority.

  • Five portals, five logics

    Five One-Stop Service portals run by different agencies: different workflows, duplicated API integrations, high compliance effort.

  • No guidance mechanism

    Investors navigated multiple agency sites with no centralized way to find the right authority for their investment.

  • No cross-agency visibility

    Slow permit processing and poor coordination. Investment facilitation was not digitally unified.

02 · Built from zero

Six foundations in six weeks

Design finalized in July, UAT by August end, official launch September 2025. Every module below designed inside that window.

  • Information architecture

    The full IA of the portal, web and mobile responsive: landing, IPA summaries, expandable service directory, FAQs, compliance.

  • Brand & design system

    Institutional identity with a WCAG-compliant color system, accessible type hierarchy and a component library serving portal and dashboard.

  • IPA Recommendation Wizard

    A rule-based decision engine: five investment questions, elimination and prioritization logic, contradiction detection, rationale-backed outputs.

  • 3-view management dashboard

    Summary KPIs, investment drilldowns and service-status views, turning a static site into a decision layer for government stakeholders.

  • PRD & vendor coordination

    Implementation-ready PRDs, real-time dev constraint resolution with Bangladesh-based teams, UAT edge cases, final QA before go-live.

  • Executive alignment

    Weekly steering updates across multiple IPAs and presentation decks that secured buy-in in near-real time.

03 · The hardest moment

Executive pushback on the wizard’s logic

Each IPA had political sensitivities around elimination logic, prioritization order and the “first recommended” position. Instead of flattening the logic, I made it transparent:

  • The pushback

    Agencies challenged any rule that could eliminate them or leave them second. Prioritization felt like ranking institutions, not guiding investors.

  • The reframe

    I exposed elimination, contradiction checks and ranking as inspectable rationale, so the recommendation was auditable, not a black box.

  • The outcome

    Steering aligned on transparent logic. The wizard shipped, and Release 1.0 launched on the September go-live date.
