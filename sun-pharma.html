Enterprise AI

Giving pharma leaders
one view of drug development

AI-powered control tower dashboards for CEOs and scientists, designed at BCGX to turn fragmented R&D data into decisions.

01 · The problem

Lack of real-time, decision-ready visibility

Scientists, PMOs, quality, manufacturing and regulatory teams ran strong processes, but decision-making stayed delayed, fragmented and reactive.

  • No unified view

    Critical information scattered across SAP, LIMS, ELN, TrackWise and spreadsheets. No single place to ask: what’s on track, what’s delayed, where do we intervene?

  • Delays were reactive

    Bottlenecks like tech transfer and approvals were identified too late, never surfaced proactively.

  • Data wasn’t actionable

    Data existed everywhere but was disconnected, unsynthesized, and never tied to a decision.

02 · Leadership

Executive Control Tower

Portfolio-level decision system for the CEO and PMO. Which projects are at risk? Where are delays emerging? What needs intervention now?

open prototype ↗

  • Designed for decisions, not reporting

    Risk signals, bottlenecks, SLA deviations and critical-path issues, not raw data. The question answered: where should I act?

  • Enabling proactive intervention

    Planned vs actual, stage-wise delays and critical-path mapping enable proactive intervention.

  • Flagging risks early

    Early warnings, delayed dependencies and escalation triggers turn hidden risks into visible decisions.

  • Proactive portfolio management

    Every project in one view with health indicators and comparative performance.

03 · Scientists

Scientist Workflow Dashboard

Task-level execution system at the bench. Know exactly what to do next, see dependencies, execute without coordination overhead.

open prototype ↗

  • Task-centric design

    A clear task queue with required inputs and outputs, not abstract workflows.

  • Workflow sequencing

    Tasks ordered logically, connected to upstream and downstream dependencies, updated automatically.

  • Contextual information

    Documents, system inputs, dependencies and constraints embedded in every task. No context switching.

  • Embedded intelligence

    Delay alerts, suggested next steps and dependency warnings support faster execution.

04 · AI in the system

Enabling decision intelligence, not automation

  • Risk detection

    Predicts delays, identifies bottlenecks.

  • Workflow insights

    Highlights inefficiencies, suggests improvements.

  • Smart alerts

    Notifies before escalation, not after.

  • Knowledge layer

    Surfaces past learnings, prevents repeat issues.
