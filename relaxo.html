GenAI

Speeding up footwear innovation
with a GenAI engine

An AI-powered innovation platform that compresses the product development cycle from months to weeks, designed at BCGX for a legacy giant turning consumer-demand-led.

  • 5x faster concept exploration
  • 15 AI-assisted lifecycle stages
  • 10–15 product directions per brief
  • weeks innovation cycle, down from months

01 · The problem

A market leader losing speed, despite its scale to rapidly evolving consumer behaviours

Decades of iconic mass-market brands, yet growth was slowing while digitally native peers accelerated on five fronts:

  • Faster innovation cycles

    Digitally native peers ship in weeks, not seasons

  • E-commerce pressure

    Discovery moved online; catalogues stayed offline

  • Premiumization

    Value brands squeezed as consumers trade up

  • Consumer-led development

    Products decided by demand signals, not gut feel

  • Data-driven merchandising

    Assortments tuned by algorithms, not tradition

How might a legacy footwear giant transform into an AI-enabled, consumer-demand-led innovation company?

02 · Key insight

From designing products to designing a system that designs products

The platform acts as a decision engine, a creative accelerator, a validation layer and a collaboration system, all at once. This makes the innovation repeatable, not dependent on intuition or individual designers. I structured the product development process around four proprietary innovation techniques, each targeting a different dimension of product creation.

  • Identity Innovation

    using Design Language

  • Functional Innovation

    using Sacrificial Concepts

  • Aesthetic Innovation

    using Shoe Swipe

  • Category Innovation

    using GenAI Engine

03 · The engine

AI Product Development Architecture

Trend signals → insights → concepts → validation → design → manufacturing → launch.

  • 01

    Trend intelligence

    AI ingests social media, street style, search trends, cultural moments and competitive launches.

  • 02

    Market & competitive analysis

    Price-density gaps, category whitespace, competitive positioning, domestic vs global opportunity.

  • 03

    Consumer persona generation

    India-specific personas with cultural context, price sensitivity and purchase triggers.

  • 04

    Concept generation

    10–15 product directions spanning silhouettes, colour palettes, design languages and naming territories.

  • 05

    Concept validation

    Synthetic consumer simulation predicts purchase intent, emotional resonance and price sensitivity.

  • 06–10

    Range planning to sourcing

    Assortment planning, SKU architecture, supplier matching, cost modelling and margin optimisation.

  • 11–15

    Production to launch

    Tech packs, sample validation, deviation detection, launch planning and marketing storytelling.

04 · Reflections

To explore and communicate the platform quickly, I also used GenAI as a design medium: prototyping complex workflows, generating interface directions, and translating abstract system ideas into tangible product experiences.
